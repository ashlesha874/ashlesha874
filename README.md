## 👋 About me

Bioinformatics researcher working on cfDNA-induced genomic instability and cancer progression using WGS and WTS multi-omics approaches. I build end-to-end NGS pipelines — from raw FASTQ to biological insight — across 20+ tumour and cell-line samples.

Currently exploring cross-species chimeric read detection, tumour microenvironment characterisation, and agentic-AI approaches for pathway analysis.

---

## 🔬 Research focus

- WGS: chimeric region detection, cross-species (human–mouse) concatemer analysis, phylogenetic correlation
- WTS: differential expression (DESeq2/edgeR), GSEA pathway enrichment, gene fusion detection
- cfDNA: genomic instability, nuclear vs extracellular DNA comparison
- Biomedical NLP: named-entity recognition on PubMed abstracts (scispaCy)
